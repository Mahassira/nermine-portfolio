# Nermine El-Behery – Portfolio

One-page site for Nermine El-Behery, a life and relationship coach in Cairo. It switches between English and Arabic.

**Live:** https://mahassira.github.io/nermine-portfolio/

It covers her story, her coaching programs, her book *Keys to Peace of Mind*, interviews, client stories, and how to reach her.

Everything is in one `index.html`, plus the images.
