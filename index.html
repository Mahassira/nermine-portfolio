I'm Nermine El-Behery

Behind my career lies a long journey of searching, changing, and understanding myself. I started out as a successful chemical engineer, then moved into education, where I developed new skills through studying the British curriculum and earning a HighScope certification. Even so, there was always an inner voice whispering that I hadn't yet reached where I truly belonged. The real turning point came when I became a Mars Venus Life Coach. That's when I started seeing life differently, and realized that changing the way we think can change the way we see the world, the decisions we make, and the relationships we build. Today, I use my coaching experience to help others pause, understand themselves more deeply, see their choices clearly, and move toward a more balanced and fulfilling life.
